# Variable mapping review

Automated mappings are intentionally conservative. Confirm these before treating any claim as applicable:

- Pulse energy is not automatically equivalent to average power or fluence.
- Frequency is not automatically equivalent to pulse overlap or duty cycle.
- Scanning speed is not automatically equivalent to translation speed under all apparatus descriptions.
- Water speed is not automatically equivalent to flow rate or pressure.
- MRR is not assumed to be derived from width, depth, and scanning speed.
